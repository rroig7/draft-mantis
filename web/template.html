      How the Mantis Score works

      The line we score

      Value per category

      Season value

      1. Replacement level is the last player a league your size would roster (teams × roster spots).
      2. Value over replacement is scaled by availability: expected games ÷ 72, softened to the 0.7 power because you can stream an injured slot.
      3. Age curve: +4% at 22 and under, −7% at 34 and over. Players marked Out lose 7%; players returning from a lost season lose 5%.
      4. Score = 50 + 50 × value ÷ best value. 100 is the top player, 50 is replacement level.

      Edge and tiers

      Edge is ESPN ADP minus Mantis rank. +15 means the room usually lets him go 15 picks after his value, so you can wait. A negative edge means you would have to reach. Tiers break where the score drops sharply, so take the last player of a tier before the run ends.

      Sources

      2026–27 projections, ADP, injury status and outlooks from the ESPN Fantasy API. Games played, age and usage from Basketball-Reference. Birth dates from the Sleeper API.
